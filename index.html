Great photos begin before the shutter clicks.

Rimona is an iPhone app for finding photo spots on a map and seeing real photos people took there. Tap Shoot like this on a photo you like, then tap it in the camera to keep it in a corner of your viewfinder as a reference. You press the shutter, then add your photo for whoever comes next.

  • Requires iOS 17 or later
  • United States and Canada
  • For ages 18 and over

How it works

Three steps, and the shutter is always yours.

  1. Find a photo spot

    Open the map to see photo spots around you. Each spot has its own page of real photos people took there.

  2. Pick a reference photo

    Tap Shoot like this on someone’s photo to bring it into your camera. Tap it there and it sits in a corner of the viewfinder while you frame your own shot.

  3. Add yours to the spot

    Post your photo to the same spot, so the next person has one more photo to learn from.

Good to know

  • Photos you post are publicThey appear on the spot’s page and on your profile, as references for other people.
  • Your camera view stays on your phoneRimona uploads a photo only after you choose to post it or choose it as your profile photo.
  • You stay in controlReport a photo, stop seeing someone’s photos, or delete your own at any time.
  • For adultsYou must be 18 or older to use Rimona.

Report a photo

Tap the photo to open it full screen, tap ••• (More) in the top right, and choose Report this photo. Then tap the reason that fits best to send your report. The person who posted the photo isn’t told who reported it.

Stop seeing someone’s photos

On a photo from someone else, tap it to open it full screen, tap ••• (More), choose Don’t show photos from this person, then tap Don’t show. You’ll stop seeing every photo from whoever posted it. To undo this later, go to Profile › Settings › Hidden people and tap Show photos.

Delete a photo you posted

Open your photo from your profile, tap ••• (More), choose Delete photo, then tap Delete. It is removed from the spot’s page and from your profile. This can’t be undone.

Delete your account

Go to Profile › Settings › Delete account and tap Delete my account. You are signed out and your photos stop being shown. Within 7 days we delete your account and the personal data stored with it, including your profile and your photos. We also ask Apple to revoke Rimona’s Sign in with Apple authorization. This can’t be undone.

If you are in a photo and did not agree to it

People who post on Rimona confirm that everyone in their photos has agreed. If you appear in a photo and did not agree, email support@lightcone.sg with the name of the photo spot and a description of the photo. You do not need a Rimona account to contact us.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Rimona is an iPhone app for finding photo spots, seeing the photos people took there, and posting your own. It is operated by LIGHT CONE PTE. LTD. (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we collect it, what we use it for, who receives it, how we retain and delete it, and the choices you have. See also the Terms of Service.

1. Data we collect and how we collect it

Data you give us

  • Sign-in. You sign in with Sign in with Apple. Apple sends us an identifier for your Apple Account and the email address you choose to share with Rimona, which can be a private relay address created by Hide My Email.
  • Profile. Your nickname, profile photo, birth date and city. You can also add a short bio and up to three photo moods. Rimona works out your zodiac sign from your birth date.
  • Photos you post. The photos you post, the photo spot you choose for them, the order you put them in and the time you posted them.
  • Searches. What you type when you search for a city or a photo spot.
  • Safety choices. Reports you send about photos, and the list of people whose photos you have chosen not to see.
  • Agreements. That you accepted our Terms of Service and this policy, and when; and that you confirmed the notice shown before your first post.
  • Messages. The emails you send us and what they contain.

Data collected automatically when you use the app

  • Location. If you allow location access, the app reads your location while you are using it. Before your location is sent to our servers, the app rounds the coordinates to three decimal places (about 110 m, or 360 ft). The rounded coordinates are what our servers receive.
  • App and connection data. An identifier that the app creates for its installation on your phone, the app version, your IP address, and technical records of the requests the app makes, such as the time, the feature used and whether it worked.

Data Rimona uses on your phone and does not upload

  • The live camera view. It is processed on your phone to show the viewfinder and your reference photo. Rimona does not upload it.
  • Your photo library. If you allow access, Rimona displays your library so that you can pick photos. You can allow all photos or only the ones you select. Section 4 explains which photos are uploaded.
  • Recently viewed photos. The list of photos you recently viewed, which the camera offers as reference photos.

2. How we use your data

We use the data above to:

  • create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in;
  • check that you are 18 or older, and work out your zodiac sign, using your birth date;
  • show your profile, and show the photos you post on the page of the spot you chose;
  • show the time of day when most photos were posted at a spot;
  • center the map, work out your current city, show photo spots near you with their distance and walking time, and suggest a spot for a photo you have just taken with Rimona’s camera;
  • show results when you search;
  • act on your safety choices: stop showing you photos from people you have hidden, and keep a record of the reports you send;
  • hide photos or accounts that break our Terms of Service;
  • keep a record that you accepted our Terms of Service and this policy, and show the notice before your first post only once;
  • keep Rimona working and protect it from misuse, for example by limiting how often photos can be posted and by finding the cause of errors;
  • reply when you email us, and find your account using the email address from Sign in with Apple.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

3. What other people can see

  • Your photos are public. People using Rimona can see the photos you post on the page of the spot you chose, and can use them as a reference when they take their own photos. Rimona shows photos without the name of the person who posted them.
  • Your nickname and profile photo. If someone chooses not to see your photos, your nickname and profile photo appear in their list of hidden people.
  • Not shown. Rimona does not show your email address, your birth date or your phone’s location to other people.

4. Location, camera and photos

  • Location. Rimona asks to use your location only while you are using the app; it does not ask for background access. If you do not allow it, the map opens on the city in your profile.
  • Camera. Rimona uses the camera so that you can take photos. A photo you take is uploaded only if you choose to post it.
  • Photo library. Rimona asks to add photos to your library when you save a photo you took, and asks to see your library when you choose photos to post. Rimona starts uploading a photo from your library only after you choose to post it or choose it as your profile photo.
  • Location inside photos. Before a photo you post is uploaded, the app converts it to a resized JPEG and removes the location information stored inside the photo file. Our servers remove location information again from the copies of the photo that are shown in Rimona. The spot shown with a photo is the one you choose; it is not read from the photo file. When you save a photo taken in Rimona to your library, the app removes its location information first.

5. Who receives your data

This section lists who receives personal data from Rimona.

  • People using Rimona see what section 3 describes.
  • Cloudflare. Cloudflare, Inc. hosts our servers, database and photo storage, and delivers photos to the app. It processes this data for us under its Data Processing Addendum, which is part of our agreement with Cloudflare. In that addendum Cloudflare agrees to process personal data only to provide its services and in accordance with our instructions, and not to use it for marketing or advertising.
  • Apple. Sign in with Apple confirms who you are when you sign in. If you delete your account, we ask Apple to revoke Rimona’s Sign in with Apple authorization. If you choose Open Apple Maps for directions, Rimona opens Apple Maps with the photo spot as the destination. To work out your city, the app sends your rounded coordinates to the geocoding service that is part of iOS and is run by Apple.
  • Google (map). The map in Rimona is provided by Google Maps, using Google software that runs inside the app and connects to Google directly from your phone. If you have allowed location access, Rimona lets the map show you where you are. Google’s terms for its maps services say that Google collects and receives data from the people who use them, including IP addresses and latitude and longitude coordinates, and the privacy disclosure Google publishes for this software lists a device identifier, crash data, performance data, usage data and other data. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google (email). Emails you send to support@lightcone.sg are received and stored in the mailbox that Google provides to us.
  • Authorities. We disclose personal data to courts, law enforcement or other authorities when the law requires it, including when we are required to report illegal content.

The companies named in this section receive personal data from Rimona under written terms: their agreements with us and their published privacy terms. We use a service only where we are satisfied that those terms give your data the same or equal protection as this policy.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • While you have an account, we keep your sign-in details, your profile, your photos, your safety choices and the record of your agreements. We use your rounded coordinates to answer the request the app is making, for example to show photo spots near you.
  • Deleting a photo. Open the photo and choose Delete photo. It is removed from the spot’s page and from your profile.
  • Deleting your account. In Rimona, go to Profile › Settings › Delete account and tap Delete my account. You are signed out and your photos stop being shown. Within 7 days we delete your account and the personal data stored with it, including your profile, your photos, any photos you deleted earlier, and any location or search data still stored with your account. We also ask Apple to revoke Rimona’s Sign in with Apple authorization.
  • Emails. We keep emails you send us for as long as we need them to deal with your request.
  • Legal requirements. If the law requires us to keep a photo or a record, for example after we have had to report illegal content, we keep it for as long as that law requires, even if the photo or the account has been deleted.

7. Your choices

  • Permissions and consent. To turn location, camera or photo access on or off, go to Profile › Settings › Permissions in Rimona, which opens Rimona’s page in iOS Settings. Turning a permission off withdraws your consent to that access. If you allowed only some photos, you can change the selection with Manage on the Choose photos screen. To withdraw your agreement to this policy, delete your account.
  • Profile. Change your nickname, profile photo, city, bio and photo moods, and turn your zodiac sign on or off, in Edit profile. Your birth date cannot be changed in the app; email us if it is wrong.
  • People. Stop seeing someone’s photos from the menu on any of their photos, and undo it in Profile › Settings › Hidden people.
  • Your data. To ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, or to ask a question about this policy, email support@lightcone.sg. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local privacy regulator.

8. Age

Rimona is for people aged 18 and over. If the birth date you enter shows that you are under 18, you cannot continue, and the nickname, birth date and city you entered are not saved.

9. Where your data is processed

LIGHT CONE PTE. LTD. is based in Singapore, and Rimona is offered in the United States and Canada. Your data may be processed outside the country where you live. We apply this policy to the personal data we hold, in whichever country it is processed. The app and our servers communicate over encrypted connections.

10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will publish the new version on this page and update the date at the top.

Rimona is a community of real photos taken at photo spots. You can browse photos on a spot page, shoot like them, and add your own photos to a spot.

2. Your account

You must be 18 or older. Sign in with Apple, keep your Apple Account secure, and tell us if you think someone else is using your account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings; within 7 days we delete your account and the personal data stored with it.
